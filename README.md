# U.S. Bank Financial Performance & Valuation Analysis

**Excel | Power BI | DAX | Financial Statement Analysis**

## Overview

This project analyzes the financial performance and equity valuation of six major U.S. banks from **2021–2025**: **JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citigroup, Goldman Sachs, and Morgan Stanley**. I standardized public financial and market data and built an interactive Power BI dashboard to compare growth, profitability, valuation, and share-price performance over time.

## Research Questions

- Which banks showed stronger revenue and net income growth over 2021–2025?
- How did **return on assets (ROA)** and **return on equity (ROE)** differ across institutions?
- How did earnings, **P/E ratios**, and year-end stock performance compare?
- What relationships appeared between changes in profitability, valuation, and market returns?

## Tools & Methods

- **Excel:** Organized and standardized annual financial and market data; checked calculations.
- **Power BI:** Built interactive visual comparisons, KPI cards, and year/bank filters.
- **DAX:** Created financial measures and comparative metrics for dashboard analysis.
- **Financial metrics:** Revenue, net income, assets, shareholder equity, ROA, ROE, diluted EPS, P/E, and annual stock returns.

## Key Findings

Complete these bullets using the **actual dashboard results** before making the repository public:

1. **Growth: JPMorgan Chase Leads Revenue and Earnings Growth** JPMorgan Chase demonstrated the strongest overall financial growth among the six banks analyzed. Revenue increased from $121.65 billion in 2021 to $182.45 billion in 2025, representing approximately 50.0% growth. Net income increased from $48.33 billion to $57.05 billion, a gain of 18.0%. JPMorgan achieved the highest revenue growth rate and net income growth rate over the five-year comparison period, although earnings fluctuated between individual years.

2. **Profitability: JPMorgan Leads, While Citigroup Lags** JPMorgan Chase recorded the strongest profitability metrics in 2025, with a 1.29% return on assets (ROA) and 17.0% return on equity (ROE). Morgan Stanley followed closely with approximately 1.19% ROA and 16.6% ROE. Citigroup exhibited the weakest profitability, reporting 0.54% ROA and 6.7% ROE in 2025. These differences highlight variations in asset efficiency and shareholder equity returns across major U.S. banking institutions.

3. **Valuation: P/E Expansion and Diverging Earnings Performance**
 Morgan Stanley recorded the highest 2025 P/E ratio at 17.11x, followed by Goldman Sachs at 16.88x and Citigroup at 16.44x. Citigroup delivered the strongest 2025 stock-price return at 70.37%, despite diluted EPS declining from $10.14 in 2021 to $6.99 in 2025. Its P/E ratio expanded from 5.06x to 16.44x over the same period. Meanwhile, JPMorgan's diluted EPS increased approximately 30.1%, from $15.39 to $20.02, demonstrating stronger earnings-per-share growth over the five-year period.

## Limitations

Bank reporting practices and revenue definitions may differ. P/E depends on the chosen EPS definition and price date. Stock-price return excludes dividends unless specifically adjusted; historical performance does not imply future returns. The dashboard is **comparative research, not an investment recommendation**.
